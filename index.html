Questions

How do I start?

Install RookEye from the Chrome Web Store, open it, and start the free trial with your email. You'll get a licence key by email: paste it into RookEye and you're in. After 7 days, choose the yearly or monthly plan to keep using it.

Which sites and browsers does it work with?

It reviews games from chess.com and lichess, games from YouTube and Twitch videos, and any game you paste as PGN. It runs in Google Chrome and other Chromium-based browsers on a computer.

Does it help during live games?

No. RookEye gives no hints during a game against a person or during rated puzzles. It only records the game so you can review it afterwards. Getting outside help during a game is cheating, and RookEye is built for learning.

How do I cancel?

Use the "Manage subscription" link in your receipt email from Lemon Squeezy. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for, and you won't be charged again.

Can I get a refund?

The 7-day free trial is there so you can try everything first, so payments aren't refunded, except where the law requires it. See the terms.

What happens to my games and data?

Your games, analysis and puzzles are kept in your browser on your computer. RookEye has no accounts and no tracking, and doesn't send your games to any server. The privacy policy lists everything it fetches and why.
